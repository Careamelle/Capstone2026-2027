Chapter 1

The Problem and Its Setting

System Architecture Demo

Abstract

This study presents a frugal engineering framework for decentralized pipeline leak detection utilizing a raw LoRa star topology. Addressing the challenge of Non-Revenue Water (NRW) in municipal systems, specifically modeled for Barangay Cabid-an, Sorsogon City, this capstone project bypasses expensive commercial LoRaWAN infrastructures. By integrating a Heltec ESP32-S3 microcontroller, a 5V analog pressure transducer, and a custom power-gating circuit, the edge node is kept strictly under a ₱2,000 budget. Data is transmitted via 915 MHz LoRa to a central receiver and visualized on a Flutter-based micro-GIS dashboard. The system evaluates pressure differentials to triangulate leaks while filtering out municipal pump shutdowns.

Background of the Study

Non-Revenue Water (NRW) due to pipeline leaks remains a critical inefficiency in municipal water distribution. Commercial leak detection systems and standard LoRaWAN architectures involve prohibitive hardware costs and recurring Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) fees, making them inaccessible for smaller local government units (LGUs). This project introduces a low-cost, decentralized alternative tailored for local water monitoring contexts. By leveraging raw LoRa topologies and edge-computing, the framework provides real-time telemetry without relying on third-party cloud gateways or expensive cellular backhauls.

Statement of the Problem

This study aims to develop a cost-effective, decentralized pipeline leak detection system. Specifically, it seeks to answer the following:

  1. How can a reliable pressure-sensing edge node be constructed for under ₱2,000 using frugal engineering principles?
  2. How can a raw 915 MHz LoRa star topology be implemented to bypass commercial gateway dependencies and fees?
  3. How can a backend algorithm accurately differentiate between isolated pipeline leaks and system-wide pressure losses, such as scheduled pump shutdowns?

Objectives

  • 1

    To design and build a bench-tested edge node combining an ESP32-S3, an analog pressure transducer, and a highly efficient MT3608 power-gating circuit.

  • 2

    To establish a raw LoRa star network for telemetry transmission to a central Firebase-integrated receiver node.

  • 3

    To develop a differential pressure algorithm in Dart capable of filtering out false positives and visualizing active leak zones dynamically on a Mapbox interface.

Scope and Limitations

This project is strictly scoped as a bench-tested proof-of-concept prototype. While the system parameters and environmental context are designed around municipal water monitoring in Cabid-an, Sorsogon City, it will not undergo live LGU utility deployment. The hardware budget is strictly capped at ₱2,000 per edge node. Furthermore, the network topology intentionally excludes commercial LoRaWAN gateways, relying entirely on a direct node-to-receiver raw LoRa configuration.
